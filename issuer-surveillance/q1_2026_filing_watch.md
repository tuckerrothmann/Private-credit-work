# Q1 2026 Filing Watch — Private Credit Surveillance Checklist

*Q1 2026 10-Qs expected: mid-May 2026 (most BDCs file 40–45 days after quarter-end). This document defines the specific marks, thresholds, and disclosures to watch for across each surveillance name when filings drop.*

---

## Why Q1 2026 is the most important filing cycle in this project

Three things happened between Q4 2025 filings and the Q1 2026 period that should show up in marks:

1. **April 2, 2026 Medallia forcing event** — Bloomberg reported lenders (led by Blackstone) refused to extend the PIK lifeline. This is the first event that falls inside the Q1 2026 measurement period (January 1 – March 31, 2026). Q1 2026 10-Qs will be the first filed data reflecting whether lenders marked to this development.

2. **Moody's Ba1 FSK downgrade (March 24, 2026)** — Falls inside Q1 2026. First time FSK has been speculative grade. FSK's Q1 filing should reflect any portfolio-level mark revisions driven by the downgrade scrutiny.

3. **Cornerstone October 2026 revolver wall** — Now within 6 months. Any revolver extension or refinancing activity in Q1 2026 should appear in filing disclosures or press releases before the Q1 10-Q drop.

## Medallia — Active restructuring; watch for non-accrual and debt write-downs

**BDC holders:** BXSL (confirmed, $307.9M / 77.75¢), FSK (confirmed, ~$454M blended / PIK tranche ~79¢), BCRED (confirmed via portfolio page)

| What to watch | Threshold that matters | Why |
|---------------|----------------------|-----|
| BXSL Q1 mark | Does it fall below 75¢? | Q4 trajectory: 94→89→87→82→77.75¢; April PIK refusal accelerates decline |
| FSK: is Medallia moved to non-accrual? | Yes/No | Moody's explicitly named Medallia in the FSK downgrade rationale; non-accrual would formally recognize what markets already price |
| FSK blended mark vs. separately disclosed PIK tranche | Does the filed blended mark begin to converge with the PIK tranche (~79¢)? | 21-point gap between FSK filed (~99¢ blended) and BXSL (77.75¢) is the largest mark dispersion in the universe |
| BCRED disclosure | Any FV or mark disclosed for Medallia? | BCRED has only listed it on portfolio page; Q1 may be first quarter with a disclosed mark given the April forcing event |
| Debt-for-equity swap / restructuring announcement | Any 8-K, press release, or SOI line showing equity conversion | This would be the resolution event; first-lien lenders receive new equity, PIK holders recover fractionally |
| Total unrealized loss disclosure | BXSL: how large is the Q1 unrealized loss on Medallia? | At 77.75¢ on $393.4M cost = ~$86M unrealized loss in Q4 2025; any further decline adds directly to BXSL NAV pressure |

**Bull case for Q1:** Clearlake / Thoma Bravo equity injection is announced; PIK refusal triggers a negotiated settlement where debt-for-equity swaps at 85¢+ give lenders recovery; BXSL mark stabilizes or improves.

**Bear case for Q1:** BXSL marks below 70¢, FSK takes Medallia non-accrual, coverage ratio deteriorates further (~0.67x as of April 2026); restructuring drags into Q2/Q3.

## Zywave (Cardinal Parent / Packers Software) — 2027 maturity wall; 99.6¢ 2L is anomalous

**BDC holders:** ARCC (confirmed — preferred written off Q3→Q4 2025, 2L at 99.6¢)

| What to watch | Threshold that matters | Why |
|---------------|----------------------|-----|
| ARCC second-lien mark | Below 95¢? Below 90¢? | November 2027 first-lien maturity is 18 months away from Q4 2025; the 99.6¢ 2L mark is anomalously high for a Caa2-rated credit with a 2027/2028 maturity wall |
| Refinancing activity | Any public announcement of 1L extension or new financing | Moody's already called the July 2025 revolver extension a distressed exchange; a repeat would be another rating event |
| Moody's rating action | Any downgrade from Caa2? | Caa2 is already deep junk; Caa3 or Ca would signal Moody's expects imminent default; any downgrade would be a market-moving event |
| Any additional BDC disclosures | Other BDCs filing positions in Cardinal Parent / Zywave | ARCC is the only confirmed holder; check OBDC, FSK, CGBD in Q1 2026 schedules |

**Core thesis:** The 99.6¢ second-lien mark is the most suspicious single data point in the current surveillance universe. A Caa2-rated credit (Moody's definition: "judged to be of poor standing and subject to very high credit risk") with a maturity wall in 18 months should NOT be marked at 99.6¢ by any reasonable valuation. Either: (a) ARCC has structural protections we're not seeing that justify the mark, or (b) the 99.6¢ will compress sharply toward 80¢ or lower in Q1–Q2 2026 as the maturity wall approaches. Watch this closely.
