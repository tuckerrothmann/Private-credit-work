# Zywave, Inc. — Issuer Surveillance Memo

*LBO entities: Cardinal Parent / Packers Software Intermediate Holdings*

## One-line thesis
Zywave is a Caa2/CCC+ insurance agency SaaS platform with a November 2027 first-lien maturity wall, ARCC's preferred equity already written to zero, and a second-lien marked at an anomalous 99.6¢ — the most likely mark-compression candidate in Q1–Q2 2026 as the refinancing window approaches without a visible exit.

## What the company does
Zywave provides software to insurance agencies, MGAs, and carriers across the U.S. independent agency distribution channel. It has assembled a broad suite through 15+ acquisitions since 2019:

| Product | Category | Origin |
|---------|---------|--------|
| BrokerageBuilder | CRM for insurance agencies | Organic |
| Partner Platform (AMS) | Full agency management system | SIS acquisition (2022) |
| DocuLink / Delivery Systems | Document distribution | Organic/acquired |
| RiskMeter | Commercial lines market intelligence | Acquired |
| Advisen | Commercial lines data and analytics | Advisen acquisition (~2021) |
| ITC | Rating engines / insurance connectivity | ITC acquisition (Dec 2020) |
| ClarionDoor | Commercial lines CPQ/rating | ClarionDoor acquisition (~$100M, Nov 2021) |
| IBQ Systems | Benefits quoting | Acquired |

Customers: ~15,000 carriers, agencies, and brokerages (per 2024 Stewardship Report — down from 18,000+ cited in 2022–2023, suggesting possible net attrition or reclassification). All top-100 U.S. insurance brokerages claimed as customers.

**Revenue:** $250–500M range (Coverager estimate); ARR ~$200–250M for the SaaS/subscription portion. The consolidated entity includes both recurring SaaS and data subscription revenues from acquired businesses.

## Sponsors
- **Aurora Capital Partners** — took Zywave private in 2019 (original LBO)
- **Clearlake Capital Group** — acquired co-sponsorship stake in October 2020 via a leveraged recap / secondary LBO that added substantial incremental debt

## Why it matters
- **Moody's Caa2, S&P CCC+** — deep distress ratings; Moody's called the July 2025 revolver extension a **distressed exchange** (de facto limited default)
- **November 2027 first-lien maturity wall** — $450–470M TLB needs to refinance or be repaid in 18 months from Q4 2025; at Caa2, a market refinancing is extremely difficult
- **ARCC preferred equity ($73.7M cost) written to zero** in Q4 2025 — the equity-layer impairment has already arrived
- **ARCC second-lien at 99.6¢** — anomalously high for a Caa2 credit; this mark is expected to compress as the 2027 wall approaches
- **ARCC is the sole confirmed BDC holder** — 19 BDCs scanned; no OBDC, BXSL, FSK, GBDC, CGBD, or others hold confirmed Zywave exposure
- Part of the **Clearlake Capital concentration** theme: Clearlake also sponsors BakeMark (Balrog) and Cornerstone OnDemand — all three simultaneously in distress/impairment in ARCC's Q4 2025 book

**Key structural insight:** Like Cornerstone OnDemand, Zywave has a broadly syndicated first-lien TLB ($450–470M) held by CLOs and institutional loan funds — the same "invisible" first-lien structure. BDC filings (ARCC) only show the junior layers: second-lien ($71.6M) and preferred (written off). ARCC holds only ~3.5% of the first-lien as a minor co-arranger allocation; the rest is in the BSL market.

**Arrangers:** BNP Paribas, Antares Capital, Ares Capital as joint lead arrangers and bookrunners. Credit Suisse and Golub Capital also participated. The Ares lead-arranger role explains ARCC's position in all three layers (1L small slice, 2L anchor, preferred 100%).

**Estimated total leverage:** ~9–13x EBITDA (Caa2/CCC+ consistent; S&P 2023 downgrade cited "financial underperformance" and negative free cash flow; Moody's stable outlook as of July 2025 implies no material improvement).

| Date | Event | Credit impact |
|------|-------|--------------|
| 2019 | Aurora Capital takes Zywave private | Original LBO; conservative initial leverage |
| October 2020 | Clearlake acquires co-sponsorship stake via leveraged recap | $340M 1L TLB (L+450, Nov 2027) + $150M 2L (Nov 2028) originated |
| December 2020 | ITC acquisition; $121.5M 1L add-on + ~$49.5M 2L | Leverage increases to 8.9x pro forma (Moody's) |
| 2021–2022 | Advisen, ClarionDoor (~$100M), SIS, IBQ, others — ~$1B total acquisition spend | Leverage increases further with each add-on |
| June 2023 | S&P downgrade to CCC+ (from B-) | Cited negative FCF, SOFR rate shock on floating L+450 debt (rate ~doubled from 5% to 9%), low liquidity; Clearlake written commitment to support company secured stable outlook |
| July 2025 | Moody's affirms Caa2, changes outlook to stable | Revolver extension completed; Moody's designates extension as **distressed exchange** (/LD notation on PDR) — revolver terms modified in a way lenders would not accept at arms-length market terms |
| Q4 2025 | ARCC writes preferred equity to zero (−$76.2M Q3→Q4) | Equity layer fully impaired; 2L still marked 99.6¢ |

**Interest rate shock:** The original L+450 with 0.75% LIBOR floor was priced in October 2020 when LIBOR was ~15bps (effective rate ~5.25%). By 2023, with SOFR at 5.30%+, the effective rate on the same spread was ~9.75% — a near-doubling of cash interest burden on a ~$450M first-lien, adding roughly $20M/year in incremental cash interest that was not in the original underwriting model.

**The 99.6¢ second-lien mark is the critical anomaly.** For context: Moody's Caa2 implies a default probability of roughly 15–25% over 12 months and expected loss rates on second-lien Caa2 credits typically imply market prices of 70–90¢. ARCC's 99.6¢ mark is either: (a) supported by structural protections or covenant package not visible in public filings, or (b) a mark that will compress sharply toward the 80–90¢ range in Q1–Q2 2026 as the November 2027 maturity wall approaches without a clear refinancing path. Given the Medallia, Cornerstone, and BakeMark precedents from this same analysis project, option (b) is the base case.

## Competitive position
- **Forrester Wave Q4 2025:** Named a "Leader" in Insurance Agency Management Systems with top score in strategy — credible product validation but not a current-performance indicator
- **Primary competitor:** Applied Epic (Applied Systems) — dominant in large commercial agency; ranked #1 in G2 for 20 consecutive quarters; Zywave targets broader mid-market
- **HawkSoft:** Significant mid-market competitor emphasizing no lock-in contracts and no data-hostage pricing — direct counter-positioning to Zywave's consolidated platform
- **Vertafore:** Most comparable PE-backed InsurTech (Roper Technologies, then TPG/Vista); similarly broad AMS + data products; potentially more resilient balance sheet
- **AgencyBloc:** Life/health/benefits agencies overlap with Zywave's IBQ benefits quoting segment
- **December 2025:** Zywave launched "Agentic AI" (four AI agents for insurance workflows) — credible positioning but revenue impact is 12–24 months away

**Customer count trend:** 18,000+ customers cited in 2022–2023 press releases vs. 15,000 cited in 2024 Stewardship Report. If the decline is real (vs. reclassification), it represents ~17% gross customer attrition — material for a SaaS platform. Cannot be confirmed without audited financials.

## November 2027 refinancing path analysis

| Outcome | Probability (qualitative) | What it means for ARCC 2L |
|---------|--------------------------|--------------------------|
| Sponsor equity injection + amend-and-extend 1L | Most likely | 2L stays near par; restructuring avoided; preferred remains zero |
| Private credit refinancing of 1L at higher spread | Moderate | SOFR+650–750bps new 1L; increased cash burden; 2L probably extended simultaneously |
| Strategic sale (Applied Systems, Vertafore, or PE buyer) | Moderate | 1L repaid at par; 2L likely near par; equity zero |
| Distressed exchange on 1L | If above fail | Moody's/S&P default event; 2L recovery uncertain; ARCC 2L mark should already be 80–90¢ range before this |
| Chapter 11 | Tail risk | Full restructuring; 2L recovery dependent on enterprise value; preferred zero confirmed |

The Clearlake written commitment to support (per S&P 2023 stable outlook) and Clearlake's track record as an active sponsor are slight positives. However, Clearlake is simultaneously managing BakeMark (Balrog, in active 1L distress at 87¢) and Cornerstone OnDemand (2L at 91–94¢) — three simultaneous Clearlake portfolio companies in distress negotiations with ARCC across different deals.

## Why it could matter to BDC unsecured bonds
ARCC's total Zywave cost basis is ~$160M, with ~$85M current FV. If the second-lien ($71.6M FV) compresses from 99.6¢ to 85¢, that's an additional ~$10M unrealized loss. At 75¢, ~$18M. These are not large numbers relative to ARCC's ~$23B portfolio, but the pattern matters: ARCC's Clearlake exposure (Zywave + BakeMark + Cornerstone) is a concentrated sponsor bet with three simultaneous deteriorating marks.

## What to watch
- **ARCC Q1 2026 second-lien mark**: Does 99.6¢ hold or compress? Any decline below 95¢ signals the market is marking to the 2027 wall
- **Any revolver or 1L refinancing announcement**: A successful 1L extension or refinancing would remove the 2027 forcing event; failure to announce one by mid-2026 increases distressed exchange probability
- **Moody's rating action**: Caa2 is already deep junk; Caa3/Ca downgrade would confirm imminent default expectation
- **S&P action**: CCC+ with Clearlake written commitment — any change to that commitment would trigger review
- **Customer count disclosure**: If Zywave publishes 2025 Stewardship Report, watch for customer count trend (15,000 baseline)
- **Clearlake portfolio management**: BakeMark + Zywave + Cornerstone simultaneous pressure on ARCC — watch for Clearlake public commentary or sponsor injection signals across all three

## Working judgment
- **Hidden mark risk: 4 / 5** — 99.6¢ second-lien on a Caa2 credit is the most anomalous mark in the surveillance universe; compression to 80–90¢ is likely within 2 quarters
- **BDC bond relevance: 3 / 5** — ARCC-only exposure limits cross-BDC spread; $160M total cost basis is meaningful but not portfolio-scale for ARCC
- **Recovery fragility: 4 / 5** — SaaS platform with integration risk, churn signals, negative FCF history; refinancing requires sponsor injection or strategic exit in a tight window
