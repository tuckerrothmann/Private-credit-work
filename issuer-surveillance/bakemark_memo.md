# BakeMark — Issuer Surveillance Memo

*LBO entity: "Balrog" (ARCC codename in Q4 2025 filings)*

## One-line thesis
BakeMark is a Clearlake-sponsored bakery ingredients distributor stressed by SOFR rate shock and commodity input cost inflation — not AI disruption — where ARCC's 88.1¢ second-lien mark diverges by 700bps from OBDC's 81¢ mark on the same instrument, the preferred equity is already written to zero, and the first-lien at 87¢ signals impairment has already breached the senior layer.

## What the company does
BakeMark distributes bakery ingredients to commercial and retail bakery customers across the United States: flour, butter, oils, sugar, and specialty items. This is a physical-distribution commodity business — no software, no recurring-revenue SaaS profile, no AI exposure. The operating model is margin-on-spread between input procurement costs and customer invoice prices, with distribution infrastructure (logistics, warehousing, customer relationships) as the primary competitive moat.

## Why it matters
- **SOFR rate shock is the primary stress driver**: ~2021 LBO at ~9x leverage with debt at L+450–550; effective rates approximately doubled by 2023 as SOFR moved from near-zero to 5%+, adding tens of millions in incremental annual cash interest burden not in the original underwriting model
- **Commodity input cost inflation compounds the operating headwind**: Flour, butter, and oils experienced significant 2021–2023 price inflation; as a distributor, margin compression occurs when pass-through to customers lags input cost increases
- **First-lien already below par at 87¢**: Unlike Zendesk (first-lien at ~100¢) or Zywave (first-lien at ~100¢), BakeMark's senior-secured layer is already marked impaired — a more advanced distress signal
- **ARCC vs. OBDC 2L mark divergence of 700bps**: ARCC marks the second-lien at 88.1¢; OBDC marks what appears to be the same instrument at ~81¢ — a 7-point gap on the same tranche is a significant red flag for at least one mark being unsustainable
- **Preferred equity written to zero**: Equity-first impairment confirmed (consistent pattern across Medallia, Zendesk, Zywave, Cornerstone OnDemand)
- **Clearlake Capital concentration**: Clearlake sponsors BakeMark, Zywave, and Cornerstone OnDemand simultaneously — all three are in distress/impairment in ARCC's Q4 2025 book; Clearlake also had Wellness Pet (distressed exchange June 2025) and Pretium Packaging (Ch. 11 2025) in the same period, raising sponsor bandwidth concerns

## Stress attribution — SOFR vs. AI

BakeMark is a deliberate contrast case to the software/AI names in this surveillance project:

| Driver | BakeMark | Software names (Zendesk, Medallia, Cornerstone) |
|--------|---------|------------------------------------------------|
| Primary stress | SOFR rate shock on 2021 floating-rate LBO | SOFR rate shock + AI disruption to recurring revenue |
| Operating headwind | Commodity input cost inflation | Revenue deceleration / churn from AI substitution |
| AI exposure | None — physical commodity distribution | High — core product workflows are AI-disruptable |
| Recovery anchor | Distribution infrastructure, inventory, customer relationships | Franchise/software value; uncertain under AI pressure |

This distinction matters for recovery analysis: BakeMark's collateral base (distribution assets, inventory, contracted customer relationships) provides a more tangible recovery floor than pure-software names where enterprise value depends entirely on forward revenue assumptions.

## Monitoring thesis

**Primary watch items:**

1. **Does ARCC 2L converge toward OBDC's 81¢?** OBDC's mark at 81¢ vs. ARCC's 88.1¢ implies one is wrong. Given OBDC typically marks junior layers more conservatively and tends to lead, any ARCC Q1–Q2 2026 2L markdown toward 83–85¢ would confirm the divergence is closing in the expected direction.

2. **First-lien mark trajectory at ARCC**: The 87¢ first-lien mark is already the most advanced senior-layer impairment in the surveillance universe (Zendesk and Zywave first-liens are still at or near par). Further compression below 85¢ would signal approaching workout territory for the senior lenders.

3. **Clearlake Capital sponsor bandwidth**: Clearlake is simultaneously managing BakeMark + Zywave + Cornerstone OnDemand distress situations with ARCC, plus Wellness Pet (distressed exchange June 2025) and Pretium Packaging (Ch. 11 2025). Sponsors with multiple concurrent workouts face real bandwidth constraints — watch for any signal that Clearlake is deprioritizing BakeMark relative to larger or higher-profile credits.

4. **Commodity input cost normalization**: Flour, butter, and oil prices have partially normalized from 2021–2023 peaks. If input costs continue to moderate, BakeMark's operating margins may recover, reducing the debt service coverage pressure. This is a potential positive catalyst absent from the AI-disruption names.

5. **SOFR path**: Any SOFR decline (rate cuts) mechanically reduces BakeMark's cash interest burden. At L+450–550, each 100bps SOFR decline reduces annual cash interest by ~4.5–5.5% of the funded debt quantum — meaningful relief if total debt is in the $500M–$1B range.

## Working judgment

- **Hidden mark risk: 4 / 5** — ARCC/OBDC 2L divergence of 700bps suggests one mark is materially wrong; ARCC preferred written off; total debt quantum unknown with no BSL secondary market to anchor marks; first-lien at 87¢ already signals advanced distress that may not be fully reflected in 2L marks
- **BDC bond relevance: 2 / 5** — position sizes are small relative to NAV ($45M combined ARCC first+second lien cost; ~$24M OBDC 2L cost); relevant primarily through the Clearlake concentration lens rather than as a standalone NAV risk
- **Recovery fragility: 3 / 5** — distribution infrastructure, inventory, and customer relationships provide some tangible collateral; commodity business recovery depends on whether input cost pressure normalizes and whether SOFR declines; less fragile than pure software names but more fragile than asset-heavy industrials
